Privacy

Your progress stays on your device. Vibe Coder: AI Flashcards has no accounts, no sign-in and no ads. It has no tracking tools either, so nothing records what you tap or how you use the app.

Your points, streaks, the cards you know and your settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us. If you want a copy, tap Export progress in Settings.

When you're online, the app gets its fonts (the styles of lettering) from Google Fonts. As with any site that uses Google Fonts, this lets Google see your device's internet address. Once the fonts are saved on your device, the app works fully offline.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may keep basic records of visits, such as your internet address. We don't get those records.

On the website, some buttons take you to other sites: Buy Me a Coffee (if you choose to support the app), the App Store and Google Play. Those sites have their own privacy rules. If you pay on Buy Me a Coffee, they handle the payment and we never see your card details.
